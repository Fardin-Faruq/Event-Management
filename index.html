Registration Instructions

  1. Enter your name and valid college email address.
  2. Provide your student ID for identification.
  3. Select an event from the available list.
  4. Check the event date, venue and registration limit.
  5. Submit the form and note your registration ID.
  6. Visit My Registrations to check your status during this session.
